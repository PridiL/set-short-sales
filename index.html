Sorting: by Short Sale Value, largest first (click a column header to re-sort) · Blue columns = calculated columns
% Change = Closing Price vs the previous trading day's close (TradingView, SET:<symbol>)
Not Covered = the part of Short Sale Volume held overnight = the increase in Outstanding vs the previous trading day (capped at that day's Short Sale Volume) · Intraday Covered = Short Sale Volume − Not Covered (shorted and bought back the same day) · Outstanding is a net figure (new shorts held overnight − buy-backs of older positions), so if older positions were covered the same day, the true Not Covered can be higher than shown and Intraday Covered lower
Total T-1 = Outstanding Short Sale Volume at the end of the previous trading day · Change (Shares) / Change (%) = Total vs Total T-1 (New = no outstanding position on the previous day)
Days to Cover = Outstanding Short Sale Volume (Total) ÷ average daily trading volume of the last 20 trading days, including that day (TradingView)
Outstanding short positions are reported to the SET by its members, who update them when clients buy back or after 60 days from the short sale · Data since 16 Nov 2026 · Updated Monday–Friday at 07:00 (Bangkok time)
